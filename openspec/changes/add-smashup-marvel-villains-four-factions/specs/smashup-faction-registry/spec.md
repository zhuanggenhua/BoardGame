## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Marvel Villain Faction Registry Entries
The Smash Up faction registry SHALL include Hydra, Kree, Masters of Evil, and Sinister Six without changing or replacing existing Marvel hero-side faction identifiers.

#### Scenario: Hero-side Marvel factions remain separate
- **WHEN** the faction registry is loaded
- **THEN** Avengers, S.H.I.E.L.D., Spider-Verse, and Ultimates retain their existing identifiers
- **AND** Hydra, Kree, Masters of Evil, and Sinister Six use newly added identifiers
- **AND** no new villain faction aliases an existing hero-side faction id.
