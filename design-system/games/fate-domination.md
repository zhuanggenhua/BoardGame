# Fate/Domination Table Style

## Surface

- Treat the supplied Fuyuki city board and card faces as the visual subject.
- Use dark ink and wood tones for the table, restrained blue-gold chrome for state, and cyan/amber accents for actionable locations and selected cards.
- Keep panels square or lightly rounded, with thin borders and clear information grouping.

## Layout

- Desktop baseline is a single tabletop: top rail, board scene, right player rail, identity strip, and bottom hand/action dock.
- Mobile keeps the same hierarchy in a vertical internal-scroll composition; map controls stay on the board and the action button remains full-width and touchable.
- Card images keep their source portrait ratio and never become CSS-drawn placeholders.

## Interaction

- Clicking a hand card selects it only during action phase.
- The eye control opens inspection without changing the domain state.
- Location anchors, identity cards, and the primary action expose disabled states from domain phase/resource rules.
